# Dashboard Guide

## Overview

The dashboard provides an executive view of NYC Yellow Taxi operations for January–March 2024.

It is built on the Gold layer of the Medallion Architecture to ensure business users query pre-aggregated data instead of raw transactional records.

---

## KPI Cards

### Total Trips

Shows the total number of taxi trips processed.

**Purpose**
- Understand business volume.
- Monitor operational demand.

---

### Total Revenue (USD)

Displays the total revenue generated by all taxi trips.

**Purpose**
- Monitor business performance.
- Compare revenue across reporting periods.

---

### Average Fare

Average fare charged per trip.

**Purpose**
- Understand customer spending patterns.
- Detect pricing anomalies.

---

### Average Trip Distance

Average distance travelled per trip.

**Purpose**
- Measure typical trip length.
- Support fleet planning.

## Why Gold Tables?

The dashboard reads only Gold tables because they contain pre-computed business metrics.

Benefits include:

- Faster queries
- Simpler SQL
- Better scalability
- Separation of analytics from ETL
